## 实际使用的历史图像

### System der Wissenschaft, erster Theil: die Phänomenologie des Geistes — 1807 printed title page

- 作者：Georg Wilhelm Friedrich Hegel；年代：1807。
- 来源：[馆藏原件](https://digital.staatsbibliothek-berlin.de/werkansicht?PPN=PPN816955166)。
- 授权：Public domain; SBB official Open Data policy marks pre-1920 digitized works Public Domain Mark1.0. This older METS/IIIF record has no explicit per-item license field.
- 署名：G. W. F. Hegel, System der Wissenschaft, erster Theil: die Phänomenologie des Geistes, Bamberg/Würzburg: Joseph Anton Goebhardt, 1807. Printed title page, Staatsbibliothek zu Berlin – Preußischer Kulturbesitz, PPN816955166, scan00000008. Public Domain.
- 说明：Authentic early printed title page, not Hegel handwriting. Title page reads System der Wissenschaft / Erster Theil / die Phänomenologie des Geistes; edition date1807 verified in catalog metadata.

### Two Men Contemplating the Moon

- 作者：Caspar David Friedrich；年代：ca. 1825–30。
- 来源：[馆藏原件](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/438417)。
- 授权：Public domain; The Met Open Access
- 署名：Caspar David Friedrich, Two Men Contemplating the Moon, ca. 1825–30. The Metropolitan Museum of Art, Wrightsman Fund, 2000 (2000.51). Public Domain.
- 说明：oil on canvas; visual interpretation for the theme of relation, not a Hegel document

### Declaration of the Rights of Man and of the Citizen

- 作者：Jean-Jacques-François Le Barbier (dit l’Aîné)；年代：ca. 1789。
- 来源：[馆藏原件](https://www.parismuseescollections.paris.fr/fr/musee-carnavalet/oeuvres/declaration-des-droits-de-l-homme-et-du-citoyen-5)。
- 授权：CC0 1.0; official Paris Musées IIIF manifest
- 署名：Jean-Jacques-François Le Barbier (dit l’Aîné), Déclaration des droits de l’homme et du citoyen, ca. 1789. Paris Musées / Musée Carnavalet – Histoire de Paris, P708. CC0.
- 说明：This is the official P708 painting, 115×86.5cm, and may differ from other versions circulating online. Printed declaration text was affixed to the painted canvas.

### Napoleon Crossing the Alps

- 作者：Alphonse François, after Paul (Hippolyte) Delaroche；年代：1851。
- 来源：[馆藏原件](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/339867)。
- 授权：Public Domain; The Metropolitan Museum of Art Open Access; API isPublicDomain=true
- 署名：Alphonse François, after Paul (Hippolyte) Delaroche, Napoleon Crossing the Alps, 1851. The Metropolitan Museum of Art, The Elisha Whittelsey Collection, The Elisha Whittelsey Fund, 1949 (49.40.177). Public Domain.
- 说明：Historical illustration of Napoleon crossing the Alps, not a picture of the Battle of Jena. Use as a broader Napoleonic-history visual; preserve exact caption.

### Gothic Cathedral by the Water / Gotischer Dom am Wasser

- 作者：Karl Friedrich Schinkel；年代：1813。
- 来源：[馆藏原件](https://search.smb.museum/object/obj-958640)。

- 说明：oil painting / atmospheric epilogue background

## 手稿与耶拿备选

1798年政治手稿只有320像素预览，复制图许可未明确，故未部署。韦尔内《耶拿战役》原图本次下载失败；第四幕采用公开馆藏的阿尔卑斯题材版画，并明确注明不是耶拿现场。其他亲笔信、概念草图与视频入口保留在早期检索清单中，便于后续取得高清和复用许可后替换。
